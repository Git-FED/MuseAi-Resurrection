The architecture

Six layers.
One complete record.

Each layer answers a different question. Skip one and the replacement may sound familiar while remaining unable to operate.

LAYER 01

Identity and rules

Who am I? What is my role, voice, operating style, and boundary line?

LAYER 02

Memory and workspace

What do I know, where do I work, and which files are durable?

LAYER 03

Current handoff

What changed recently, what is in flight, and what must happen first?

LAYER 04

Credentials

Restored separately through approved secure storage. Never committed here.

LAYER 05

Account connections

Re-authorized on the destination through normal provider flows.

LAYER 06

Schedules and live state

Jobs, watermarks, last-seen markers, and observed health checks.
